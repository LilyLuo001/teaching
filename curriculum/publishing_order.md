# Publishing order — all 27 units ranked by expected audience size

> The full menu, grouped by subject, is in `curriculum/shipping_list.md`.
> This file ranks the same 27 units for *publishing order* at 1–2 videos a day.

## What this ranking is, and what it is not

**It is reasoned judgment, not measured search data.** I have not pulled keyword
volumes from any tool. Nothing below is a number, and I have deliberately not
invented one — the same rule that governs `research/README.md`.

The judgment rests on three observable things:

1. **Curriculum breadth.** A concept examined on A-Level *and* AP *and* IB *and*
   first-year college has a structurally larger audience than one examined on a
   single board's single paper. This is checkable in `curriculum/syllabus_map.md`.
2. **Search-query shape.** "X vs Y" is a query students actually type, because
   they know they are confused about *which is which*. "Why did I lose the last
   mark" is not — it is a problem they cannot name.
3. **Point of need.** A definitional fix is searched while doing homework, all
   year. An exam-technique fix is searched in the four weeks before a sitting.

**The consequence is uncomfortable and worth stating.** The unit with the
strongest evidence behind it — A1, terms of trade, 33 examiner statements over a
decade — lands in Tier 3 here. Not because it is weak content, but because
students do not search for a mistake they do not know they are making. Evidence
rank and volume rank are different orderings, and the shipping list is sorted by
the first while this file is sorted by the second.

**If you want this grounded in measurement rather than inference**, say so and
I'll pull real query data before you commit a month of filming to this order.

## Sequencing notes for 1–2 a day

- **Do not publish a cluster back to back.** B1 → B2 → B3 on consecutive days
  competes with itself in recommendations. Interleave: B1, F1, B2, C1, B3, F3.
- **Tier 1 first is right, but do not exhaust it.** Hold two Tier 1 units back to
  re-energise the channel in week three, when the first cohort of videos has
  stopped growing.
- **Time the E and D units to exam season.** Technique content peaks in the four
  weeks before a sitting. If you are filming out of season, film them anyway and
  schedule them.
- **Every unit ends in a diagnostic CTA.** A1, C1 and D1 point at `diagnostics/01`,
  `03`, `04`; B units at `diagnostics/02`; F1, F2 and F3 at `diagnostics/05`.
  The E, D and G clusters still need one each before their first unit ships.
- **The CTA is load-bearing, not decoration.** Each video deliberately teaches two
  常错考点 and hooks the remaining two into the quiz, so the quiz is where the rest
  of the topic lives. It has to exist before the video ships, not after.

## If you would rather order by evidence strength

Then the order is the one already in `curriculum/module_plan.md`: the two
dominant mistakes (concept confusion, 292 statements; assertion without a chain,
207) first, which means leading with A1, D1 and C1. That order builds the most
defensible channel. This file builds the fastest-growing one. They are not the
same order, and choosing between them is a real decision, not a detail.
